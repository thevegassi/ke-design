The Key Entertainment logo — use it for any header, cover, thumbnail or social frame that needs the brand signature.

```jsx
<Logo height={60} color="var(--ke-oxford-blue)" />
<Logo variant="mark" height={120} color="var(--ke-lime)" />
```

- Clear space is **1/3 of the logo height on all four sides** (brandbook p.03). Nothing else enters it.
- Approved combinations: black on white · white on black · **black on lime** · oxford blue on tropical indigo · **white on oxford blue** · white on a photo darkened to 50%.
- **Two combinations drawn in the source are withdrawn on contrast grounds:** white on lime (1.5:1) and black on oxford blue (1.6:1). The wordmark's hairline strokes vanish in print and at small sizes. On lime the logo is always black; on oxford blue it is always white.
- Never colour the mark and wordmark differently.
- `variant="mark"` is the compact version. It carries the same 1/3 clear space and the same colour rules.
