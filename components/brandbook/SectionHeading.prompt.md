The page heading used on every interior brandbook page — a 14×60px accent bar, 30px gap, Nunito Sans Bold title.

```jsx
<SectionHeading>Основные цвета</SectionHeading>
<SectionHeading rule="#FFFFFF" size={72}>Логотип</SectionHeading>
```

Sits at x=108 / y=108 on a 1920×1080 page. The bar is tropical indigo on white and off-white grounds, white on periwinkle grounds. Titles are 48px on interior pages, 72px on chapter openers.
