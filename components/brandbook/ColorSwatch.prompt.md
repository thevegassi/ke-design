The colour specimen panel used on the brandbook's core-colour page — one panel per brand colour, three across a 1704px page with a 30px gap.

```jsx
<ColorSwatch hex="#D3FF33" name="lime" cmyk="17 0 80 0" rgb="211 255 51" />
<ColorSwatch hex="#0C2350" name="oxford blue" cmyk="100 33 0 80" rgb="0 34 51" />
```

The hex headline is always oxford blue; the name and build lines are white at full and 50% opacity. Radius 54px, padding 60px — do not substitute other radii.
