✕ white on lime — do not use

The brand file lists white-on-lime among its logo colour combinations (brandbook p.05), but white on #D3FF33 measures a 1.5:1 contrast ratio — the wordmark's thin strokes disappear in print and at small sizes. It is withdrawn here. On lime, set the logo in black. Oxford blue on tropical indigo (taken from the logo colour sheets) replaces it in the approved set.
